- 2026-11-25, QR25 review: The scene's quaternion normalisers sum the squares left to right and multiply by the reciprocal length: `crates/ferroan-scene/src/xml_read.rs` `normalize_quat` (line 796), `crates/ferroan-scene/src/objects.rs` `loaded_rotation` (line 897) and the Z/W pair in `crates/ferroan-scene/src/terse.rs` (line 313). The `full_rotation` branch in `terse.rs` (line 305) does not normalise at all, where C# does (`LLClientView.cs` around line 7060 at `c401bceecd`). libopenmetaverse `a3596616c1` `Quaternion.Normalize` (`OpenMetaverse.Types/Quaternion.cs` 264–280) on x86-64 uses the pairwise `Sse41.DotProduct` and divides each component by the length. The two differ in the last bit about 64% of the time, in OAR object XML and prim `ObjectUpdate` rotations. Not fixed in QR25: one shared helper and an oracle batch through `Normalize()` are a separate item.
