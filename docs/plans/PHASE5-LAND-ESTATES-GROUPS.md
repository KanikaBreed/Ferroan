# Phase 5 plan, part 1: land, estates, groups, inventory transfer and money

Planning note for the manager. This file is the map. It does not add rows to [TASKS.md](../../TASKS.md) or [WORK_QUEUE.md](../../WORK_QUEUE.md). The manager allocates queue ids after review.

**Gate** ([PLAN.md](../PLAN.md)): Phase 5 is land and parcels, estates, groups in the region, inventory transfer, the money module, NPCs and bots, linkset data, Experiences, map tiles, dynamic textures, OAR and IAR, media, sound, remote admin, and monitoring. This file is the first slice of that sentence. The later parts of Phase 5 are named below and are not scheduled here.

**Scope here:** parcel editing beyond the Phase 2 read path (subdivide, join, buy, sell, deed, return, ban and access lists, experience entries on those lists, ban enforcement, and the land tools' packets and caps); estates (the viewer tools and dialogs, managers, bans, the covenant, region and estate settings written from the viewer, the estate experience lists, terrain upload, download and bake, the map-refresh command, the estate console commands, and the region-to-region estate handler); groups on the region (the in-process groups service and its SQLite `os_groups` writes, roles, powers, notices, region-side group chat, group land and objects, the group caps, and the region side of Groups V2 over the Local and Remote connectors); inventory transfer (give one item, give a folder, the offer, accept and decline, including an offer carried by an instant message); and the region's money module (the hooks a buy and a payment call, the module Tranquillity ships, the upload cap's price, `RequestPayPrice`, the currency HTTP endpoints, and the result when no money module is registered).

**Later Phase 5 parts, not this file:** NPCs and bots, linkset data, the Experiences caps and key-value store, world-map tile drawing, dynamic textures, saving an archive, IAR round trips, media playback, sound, remote admin, and monitoring. A media field that arrives on a parcel update is stored and sent back with the parcel. Playback stays with the media part (part 2, P5.71). Directory search of groups is the Phase 6 search work (P6.20, P6.22). P6.20 calls `GroupsModule.FindGroups`, which is P5.23i. An inventory offer to someone who is offline is P6.30 and P6.31.

## 1. What Tranquillity does today

A viewer, a script, another simulator or a grid service can observe the packets, caps, instant messages, console lines, ini keys and stored rows below. Refusals are silent except where an alert string is quoted. Those strings are part of the result.

`JoinDivideAllowed` (1331–1336): when `RegionSettings.AllowLandJoinDivide` is on, any caller passes that first check. When it is off, only an estate manager or an administrator does. The parcel power `GroupPowers.LandDivideJoin` is a second check, through `CanEditParcelProperties`.

`CanEditParcelProperties` (`PermissionsModule.cs` 1405–1410) takes `allowManager` and then calls `GenericParcelOwnerPermission` with that argument hardcoded false. Every `UpdateLandProperties` group passes false anyway. The callers that pass true are `LandSetSale` (`LandObject.cs` 566), freeze and eject (`LandManagementModule.cs` 2565 and 2608; eject still admits an administrator at 2608–2609), object owners (1722), select (`LandObject.cs` 1745) and the environment (`World/LightShare/EnvironmentModule.cs` 571, commented "wrong"). An estate manager who is not the owner and not an administrator fails those edits. Section 5 asks whether Ferroan reproduces that. It is TB233.

`UpdateLandProperties` (528–722) gates each field group on `CanEditParcelProperties` and one `GroupPowers` bit. A landing point is applied when it is zero, or finite and inside the parcel (`LandObject.cs` 619–632). A point outside the parcel leaves the stored landing type, location and look-at as they were. `ObscureMusic` is forced false (650). `ObscureMedia` is taken from the MOAP flag (652). While the estate is `TaxFree`, pass hours, pass price, and the access and ban flag deltas are skipped (661–685). A power that changes fields and no flag still stores. Three further rules show up in a capture:

- A new `GroupID` is taken only when the editor is a member of that group, an administrator, or is clearing it (583–590).
- Stored estate `DenyMinors` and `DenyAnonymous` force the matching parcel flags on every update (688–698).
- When the parcel holds a ban entry, `UseBanList` is forced on (710–711).

The cap applies `see_avs`, `any_av_sounds` and `group_av_sounds` only when `see_avs` is present (2169–2174). When that key is absent, all three are set true (2175–2179). Media description, type, size and loop are copied onto the update (2159–2163). Playback is a later part. The HTTP results are 200 with an empty body, 400 for bad LLSD, 404 for an unknown parcel or a non-POST, 410 when the client is gone, and 500 when the handler throws (2083–2188).

A sale writes `AuthBuyerID`, `SalePrice` and the `ForSale` bit under `LandSetSale`. `SellParcelObjects` alone is not a sale: the validator at 1842–1845 says the viewer's cancel leaves that flag set, and treating it as a sale would give the parcel away. Buy succeeds only when `ForSale` is set, the offered price covers `SalePrice`, the authorised buyer is zero or the buyer, `CanResell` allows the current owner (1870–1874), and a group buy has `GroupPowers.LandDeed` through `HasGroupPowers` (1877–1881, 1850–1852). `CanResell` is false when the region disallows land resale and the owner is not the estate owner, an estate manager or an administrator. The transfer (1806–1808) also requires `economyValidated`, and `amountDebited` covering the price when the price is above zero. Those two fields are the money module's. With no money module the buy does not complete, including a free parcel.

Deed (1884–1900) goes only to the parcel's current `GroupID`. `CanDeedParcel` is the permission check. Abandon and reclaim set the owner to the estate owner. Reclaim also turns the three avatar-visibility fields back on.

`ClientOnParcelAccessListUpdateRequest` (683–720) accepts flags 8 and `0x10` inside the `0x1B` mask. Flag 8 requires `LandManageAllowed`. Flag `0x10` requires `LandManageBanned`. Those entries are the experience allow and block lists on the parcel. Part 2 reads them. The write is this file.

A pass with `cost != 0` and a money module takes the money path (633–661). Otherwise the access entry is added (663–668), so a priced pass with no money module is granted. The alerts on the money path are `You already have access to parcel`, `You already have a pass valid for {0:0.###} hours`, the minutes form, the seconds form (606–621), `Insufficient funds in region '{name}' money system` (644) and `Sorry pass payment processing failed, please try again later` (652). Section 5 asks whether Ferroan keeps the free grant.

`EnforceBans` (358–432) alerts `You are banned from parcel, please leave by your own will` or `You do not have access to the parcel` or `You are banned from parcel`, then `ForceAvatarToPosition`. A god is left where they stand (436). Entering a parcel runs the check (449–465). Significant movement sends one ban-line or restricted-line properties packet when the lines are on and the estate is not `TaxFree` (522–529, 469–489). No Phase 2 row owns that enforcement.

`ClientOnParcelSelectObjects` (1705–1708) indexes `m_landList[local_id]` with no lookup and no lock. An unknown local id throws. That is TB234.

`[LandManagement]` is read at 131–149. `LimitParcelLayerUpdateDistance` defaults true, `ParcelLayerViewDistance` 128, `DefaultAdministratorParcelName` `Admin Parcel`. `ShowParcelBansLines` starts true. `BanLineSafeHeight` starts at 100. `DisableParcelBans` defaults false, and turning bans off also forces the ban lines off (147–148). Ban lines are drawn when the show flag is on and the estate is not `TaxFree` (528). The same keys are in `RegionServerDefaults.ini` under `[LandManagement]`. `RegionServer.ini.example` comments `ShowParcelBansLines`.

`land clear` asks for confirmation and, on `y`, removes every parcel. `land show` prints the table, and `land show` with a local id prints one parcel. A bad id prints `No parcel found with local ID` and that id.

Deeding an object to a group, and a god set-owner, are `Scene.ObjectOwner` (`Scene.cs` 3562 subscribes it; the body is `Scene.Inventory.cs` 3581–3652). A non-god with a non-zero owner id returns. A zero owner id deeds the object when `CanDeedObject` passes. `ObjectSaleInfo` (`World/Objects/BuySell/BuySellModule.cs` 76–105) sets the sale type and the price that an object buy then checks.

`GodUpdateRegionInfo` and `SimWideDeletes` are dispatched (`LLClientView.cs` 8739, 8751) and no module subscribes those events at Pin9. This plan leaves them unbuilt.

### Estates

The region module is `EstateManagementModule` (`Source/OpenSim.Region.CoreModules/World/Estate/EstateManagementModule.cs`). Robust estate storage is P1.12. Phase 2 already sends the handshake and loads the estate. This slice is the viewer and console writes, the dialog reads, the terrain file transfer, and the resend those writes perform.

`EstateModule` (`World/Estate/EstateModule.cs` 67–96) is on when `[Estates]` is absent, and the shipped defaults have no `[Estates]` section. It registers `EstateSimpleRequestHandler` at `/estate` (`EstateRequestHandler.cs` 51). The methods are `update_covenant`, `update_estate`, `estate_message`, `teleport_home_one_user` and `teleport_home_all_users` (113–125). `EstateConnector` (59–134 and 203–205) POSTs those forms, with a `TOKEN` field, to `{ServerURI}estate` on every region of the estate. A covenant change (`EstateModule.cs` 164–183), an estate list change after the idle store (`OnEstateInfoChange` 205–212, fired from `EstateManagementModule.cs` 873), an estate message, and a teleport home go to the estate's other regions. `OnEstateMessage` (216–236) notifies every scene of that estate in this process, then the connector posts. The handler is P5.17b. The connector is P5.17c. The override at `EstateManagementModule.cs` 1665–1670 means the local home and kick body never runs on a stock region: `EstateModule` is the path that runs. Section 5 asks whether Ferroan serves `/estate`, and with which token. The handler's default token is TB235, one sentence in section 7.

`Initialise` (85–96): `AllowRegionRestartFromClient` defaults true. When `[EstateManagement]` exists, `IgnoreEstateMinorAccessControl` and `IgnoreEstatePaymentAccessControl` default true. `RegionLoaded` (118–124) sets `DoDenyMinors` and `DoDenyAnonymous` from the inverse of those ignores, so a shipped region keeps the stored bits and does not enforce them. The matching comment block is `RegionServerDefaults.ini` 636–645.

## 3. Proving it against C#

Every row closes on a C# result. A Ferroan round trip is not the oracle. The comparison is one of these:

- A capture. Run the viewer packet or the cap against a Pin9 region and keep the datagram, the event-queue body or the HTTP status and body. Ferroan must produce those bytes. Where a fixture redacts a secret, re-encoding the unredacted original has to reproduce the capture before the redaction.
- An oracle. A small compile or a replay of the C# decision (the land-buy predicate, the charge properties, the estate cap, the group-invite refusal) with the same inputs, compared as values.
- A store swap, once P2.9a, P2.0e4.w1, P2.0e4.w2 and P2.0e4.w4 have landed. Edit on Ferroan, stop, open the same file with a Pin9 region, and the viewer sees the same parcel, the same estate lists and the same region settings. The other direction starts from a Pin9 edit. Until those writers exist, the row closes on the packet and on the in-memory land, estate or group the same process would send. The evidence column names that packet close, and names the store swap that waits on the writers.
- A live run with Ferrobot or `ferroan-bot` against a Pin9 region, for a packet the bot can send (buy, pass, give, balance, group activate). The captured reply is the fixture. The bot is the sender. It is not the specification. An inventory offer needs both: Ferrobot and `ferroan-bot` under D17.
- Groups Local: the reply packets, and the `os_groups` rows `GroupsService` wrote through `SQLiteGroupsData`, against Pin9's local connector on a copy of the same store.
- Groups Remote: the POST form (`METHOD`, the fields, `AuthType`, and the key header only when the key is set) and the `POST /groups-changed` body, against Pin9's remote connector. A C# Robust (P1.10) can sit on the other end of that capture. Ferroan does not reimplement it.
- `/estate`: the form a Pin9 region posts, and the result a Pin9 handler returns for the same form. The token is a configured value. The capture redacts it.

Risks:

- The parcel field matrix is wide, and the UDP update and the cap update do not write the same set. A row that tests one path and claims both will miss `see_avs` and the media fields. A landing point outside the parcel stays stored.
- Deed, abandon, reclaim and buy send properties to every presence. A fixture that counts only avatars over the parcel will disagree on sequence −10000.
- `CanEditParcelProperties` ignores `allowManager`. Reproducing it means an estate manager's edit fails where the viewer may still offer the control. Section 5 asks before that is locked.
- Estate list updates are queued and stored after the worker is idle. A test that reads the store in the same instant will flake. The caps (20, 500, 63, 500) are part of the result. The experience lists use the same idle store.
- A stock region serves `/estate`. A C# region that shares an estate with a Ferroan region will POST to it. Section 5 asks whether Ferroan answers.
- Two group connectors must produce the same viewer packets. Local is the in-process service. Treating it as a client of a service this plan does not build will leave create, invite and eject with nothing behind them.
- The group invite is stored by P5.26, with no reply packet. The `GroupInvitation` instant message, including to an avatar in the same region, is P5.26b, and that row depends on P4.30.
- Off-region inventory offers wait on P4.30. Building a private sender inside P5.35 would split the instant-message path.
- The shipped money module grants free actions and refuses a priced object. The group-create comment and the code default disagree (TB218). The land comment claims a parity with objects that the object path does not have (TB220). A priced pass with no money module is granted. Section 5 asks before that grant is changed.
- Restart proof for parcels and estates waits on the Phase 2 writers. That wait is on the calendar. It does not make the protocol work free, and it does not move the writer into this phase.
- A land return that deletes by a new path will disagree with Take on the trash folder and on `KillObject`.
- TB152 stays on the properties send. Storing estate deny bits here does not put them into `ParcelProperties` until that bug's own decision says so.
- TB213 and D145 stay on the estate writer. P5.15 follows D145 once it has landed.

## 5. Questions for the maintainer

Each one is open. The recommendation is the reading of the pin. It is not a decision.

1. **What does the first Ferroan region ship for money?** The stock region loads `BetaGridLikeMoneyModule`: balance 0, free actions succeed, a priced object buy is refused, a priced land buy is marked paid with no ledger, and naming any other module registers nothing. Recommendation: ship that observable behaviour. Leave `DTLNSLMoneyModule` and the money server on P1.15.

2. **Which groups service does the Ferroan region run for Local, and who builds it?** Local is `GroupsService` in the region process, with `GroupRules` and SQLite `os_groups` (`SQLiteGroupsData`, `groups.db`). P1.10 is the Robust front and its MySQL and PostgreSQL stores, and it does not write those SQLite tables. `GroupsServiceLocalConnectorModule` (66) and `GroupsServiceRobustConnector` (67) construct the same class. Recommendation: this plan builds the Local front and the SQLite writes (P5.23b, P5.23d, P5.23e, P5.23f). Whichever of P5.23b and P1.10 lands first builds `GroupsService`, and the other wraps it. Remote proofs use a C# Robust and name P1.10 as that dependency. They do not build the Robust front.

3. **Is the Hypergrid groups connector in this slice?** The example ini names it. The shipped region does not load it. Recommendation: leave it out. A later part can add it beside the Remote form.

4. **Does region-side group chat ship on?** This plan owns `Groups Messaging Module V2` (P5.23c, P5.23g, P5.23h). The shipped region leaves `MessagingModule` unset, so the module does not start. Recommendation: match that default. The rows are the module, turned on when the key names it. Notices stay on P5.28 either way. P1.10's group-chat check with Ferrobot runs against a C# region and does not wait on these rows.

5. **Do the estate console commands ship with the region?** They are registered by the estate module the scope names, and the commands that complete save and resend the handshake. Recommendation: include them (P5.18c). A short command, and a bad number, throw (TB219). What Ferroan prints is question 19.

6. **Does a priced buy wait on a money module that debits?** The shipped module does not debit. With no module, a land buy does not complete and an object buy sends no alert. Recommendation: match those two results. Do not add a ledger in this slice.

7. **Do instant-message inventory offers wait on P4.30?** The copy into a local inventory does not. Delivery to a recipient outside the region does. The group invite message is a different packet and does wait, including for a local invitee (P5.26b). The stored invite is P5.26. Recommendation: P5.32 to P5.34 proceed. P5.35 waits on P4.30 and does not grow a second sender.

8. **Does parcel-edit persistence wait on the Phase 2 writers?** D39 leaves the ordinary-path write as the default parcel, and P2.0e4.w1 is the non-default parcel and its access list. Recommendation: do not override D39. Call those writers. Prove packets and in-memory land before they land. Restart proof stays on their far side.

9. **Do script land, money and give calls move into this phase?** They are P4.S6, P4.S7, P4.S9, P4.S14, P4.S15 and P4.S19. Recommendation: leave the function bodies there. This file is the region behaviour they call. `osSetRegionWaterHeight` reaches `ChangeWaterHeight` (P5.18b). P4.F3.5 is `llInstantMessage` and is not one of these callers.

10. **TB217.** A buy of a missing local id, or a buy from an agent who is not a live root, throws before `Unable to buy now. The object was not found.` Recommendation: send that alert and do not throw. A throw is not a result a viewer depends on. The other refusal texts stay as Pin9 sends them.
