# Phase 5 plan, part 1: land, estates, groups, inventory transfer and money

Planning note for the manager. This file is the map. It does not add rows to [TASKS.md](../../TASKS.md) or [WORK_QUEUE.md](../../WORK_QUEUE.md). The manager allocates queue ids after review.

**Gate** ([PLAN.md](../PLAN.md)): Phase 5 is land and parcels, estates, groups in the region, inventory transfer, the money module, NPCs and bots, linkset data, Experiences, map tiles, dynamic textures, OAR and IAR, media, sound, remote admin, and monitoring. This file is the first slice of that sentence. The later parts of Phase 5 are named below and are not scheduled here.

**Scope here:** parcel editing beyond the Phase 2 read path (subdivide, join, buy, sell, deed, return, ban and access lists, and the land tools' packets and caps); estates (the viewer tools and dialogs, managers, bans, the covenant, region and estate settings written from the viewer, and the estate console commands); groups on the region (roles, powers, notices, group land and objects, and the region side of Groups V2 over the Local and Remote connectors); inventory transfer (give one item, give a folder, the offer, accept and decline, including an offer carried by an instant message); and the region's money module (the hooks a buy and a payment call, the module Tranquillity ships, and the result when no money module is registered).

**Later Phase 5 parts, not this file:** NPCs and bots, linkset data, Experiences (including the estate experience-list delta), map tiles (including the estate map refresh and terrain-file upload), dynamic textures, saving an archive, IAR round trips, media playback, sound, remote admin, and monitoring. A media field that arrives on a parcel update is stored and sent back with the parcel. Playback stays with the media part. Group chat (`Groups Messaging Module V2`) ships off and is a question in section 5. Directory search of groups is the Phase 6 search work.

**Grid services stay on their Phase 1 rows.** Groups V2 as a Robust service is P1.10. Estates and Experiences over Robust are P1.12. The money server, and the region module that talks to it, are P1.15. This file is the region calling those services when an operator turns them on. It does not replace them.

## 1. What Tranquillity does today

A viewer, a script, another simulator or a grid service can observe the packets, caps, instant messages, console lines, ini keys and stored rows below. Refusals are silent except where an alert string is quoted. Those strings are part of the result.

`JoinDivideAllowed` (1331–1336): when `RegionSettings.AllowLandJoinDivide` is on, any caller passes that first check. When it is off, only an estate manager or an administrator does. The parcel power `GroupPowers.LandDivideJoin` is a second check, through `CanEditParcelProperties`.

`CanEditParcelProperties` (`PermissionsModule.cs` 1405–1410) takes `allowManager` and then calls `GenericParcelOwnerPermission` with that argument hardcoded false. An estate manager who is not the owner and not an administrator fails the parcel edit even where the land module passed true. Section 5 asks whether Ferroan reproduces that.

`UpdateLandProperties` (528–685) gates each field group on `CanEditParcelProperties` and one `GroupPowers` bit. A landing point must be finite and inside the parcel, or it is cleared. `ObscureMusic` is forced false (650). `ObscureMedia` is taken from the MOAP flag (652). While the estate is `TaxFree`, pass hours, pass price, and the access and ban flag deltas are skipped (661–685). A power that changes fields and no flag still stores.

The cap applies `see_avs`, `any_av_sounds` and `group_av_sounds` only when `see_avs` is present (2169–2174). When that key is absent, all three are set true (2175–2179). Media description, type, size and loop are copied onto the update (2159–2163). Playback is a later part. The HTTP results are 200 with an empty body, 400 for bad LLSD, 404 for an unknown parcel or a non-POST, 410 when the client is gone, and 500 when the handler throws (2083–2188).

A sale writes `AuthBuyerID`, `SalePrice` and the `ForSale` bit under `LandSetSale`. `SellParcelObjects` alone is not a sale: the validator at 1842–1845 says the viewer's cancel leaves that flag set, and treating it as a sale would give the parcel away. Buy succeeds only when `ForSale` is set, the offered price covers `SalePrice`, the authorised buyer is zero or the buyer, `CanResell` allows the current owner (1870–1874), and a group buy has `GroupPowers.LandDeed` through `HasGroupPowers` (1877–1881, 1850–1852). `CanResell` is false when the region disallows land resale and the owner is not the estate owner, an estate manager or an administrator. The transfer (1806–1808) also requires `economyValidated`, and `amountDebited` covering the price when the price is above zero. Those two fields are the money module's. With no money module the buy does not complete, including a free parcel.

Deed (1884–1900) goes only to the parcel's current `GroupID`. `CanDeedParcel` is the permission check. Abandon and reclaim set the owner to the estate owner. Reclaim also turns the three avatar-visibility fields back on.

`[LandManagement]` is read at 131–149. `LimitParcelLayerUpdateDistance` defaults true, `ParcelLayerViewDistance` 128, `DefaultAdministratorParcelName` `Admin Parcel`. `ShowParcelBansLines` starts true. `BanLineSafeHeight` starts at 100. `DisableParcelBans` defaults false, and turning bans off also forces the ban lines off (147–148). Ban lines are drawn when the show flag is on and the estate is not `TaxFree` (528). The same keys are in `RegionServerDefaults.ini` under `[LandManagement]`. `RegionServer.ini.example` comments `ShowParcelBansLines`.

`land clear` asks for confirmation and, on `y`, removes every parcel. `land show` prints the table, and `land show` with a local id prints one parcel. A bad id prints `No parcel found with local ID` and that id.

### Groups in the region

The shipped region leaves groups off. `RegionServerDefaults.ini` 2689–2694 sets `[Groups] Enabled = false` and `Module = Default`. Notices, the messaging module and the services connector are commented out.

`Addons/OpenSim.Addons.Groups/GroupsModule.cs` `Initialise` (61–83): a missing `[Groups]` section returns. `Enabled` defaults false (72). `Module` must equal this module's `Name`, `Groups Module V2` (78, 215–217), or the module returns. `NoticesEnabled` defaults true (87). `LevelGroupCreate` defaults 0. When the module is on it registers `IGroupsModule` (94–98). The core stub in `Source/OpenSim.Region.CoreModules/Avatar/Groups/GroupsModule.cs` is a different class (`Name` `GroupsModule`). The deprecated Flotsam module loads only when `Module` is `GroupsModule`. This slice follows Groups V2.

`LLClientView` takes `IGroupsModule` at construction (529). Each group handler returns with no reply when that reference is null. The handlers are `HandleActivateGroup` (11807), `HandleGroupTitlesRequest` (11847), `HandleGroupProfileRequest` (11882), `HandleGroupMembersRequest` (11942), `HandleGroupRoleDataRequest` (11988), `HandleGroupRoleMembersRequest` (12025), `HandleCreateGroupRequest` (12071), `HandleUpdateGroupInfo` (12089), `HandleSetGroupAcceptNotices` (12106), `HandleGroupTitleUpdate` (12118), `HandleParcelDeedToGroup` (12127), `HandleGroupNoticesListRequest` (12136), `HandleGroupNoticeRequest` (12170), `HandleGroupRoleUpdate` (12179), `HandleGroupRoleChanges` (12199), `HandleJoinGroupRequest` (12216), `HandleLeaveGroupRequest` (12225), `HandleEjectGroupMemberRequest` (12234) and `HandleInviteGroupRequest` (12248). Account, proposal and vote packets raise events the V2 module does not subscribe.

What the viewer gets back:

- Activate, a title change, and an agent-data request: `AgentDataUpdate`, and event-queue `GroupMembershipData` (UDP `AgentGroupDataUpdate` when the client has no caps).
- `UUIDGroupNameRequest`: `UUIDGroupNameReply`.
- Profile, members (25 a packet), role data, role members (32 pairs a packet), titles: the matching reply, or nothing for a zero profile id or a repeat inside 10 seconds.
- Create: `CreateGroupReply`. The level check is 984–989. The money check is 992–1002, and `ApplyCharge` runs only when `GroupCreationCharge` is greater than 0 (1010–1011). With no money module the charge is skipped and create continues.
- Role create, update and delete: `GroupRoleUpdate` (1073). A new role is stored under `UUID.Random()`, not the packet's role id (1081–1083). The powers `ulong` is forwarded. The service interprets it. Create failure is an alert. `NotifyChange` refreshes members who are in the region.
- Role membership changes: `GroupRoleChanges`, change 0 adds and change 1 removes.
- Join and leave: `JoinGroupReply` and `LeaveGroupReply`. Leave's reply is true and the client is dropped from the group.
- Eject: `EjectGroupMemberReply`. Success is the membership reading back as gone (1274–1291), and a refused ejection replies false. A local ejectee gets an instant message and a group drop. A root on another region is sent dialog 210 (654, 728, 1360).
- Invite: no reply packet. The instant message uses dialog `GroupInvitation`. The region comment at 1422 says the security check is still to do. The service already refuses the invite. `GroupsService.AddAgentToGroupInvite` (555–573) rejects a banned invitee, an existing member, and a requester who lacks `GroupPowers.Invite` or who cannot assign the role. That comment is stale. It is not a new bug.
- Notices: an instant message, dialog `GroupNotice`, not a packet. The region refuses it when notices are off, when the sender has no membership, or when `GroupPowers.SendNotices` is missing. The body is `subject|message`. A list request gets `GroupNoticesListReply`. Opening one sends dialog `GroupNoticeRequested`.

Accept and decline of an invite, and of a notice attachment, arrive as instant messages (`OnInstantMessage` 358, 535, 589). Accepting an invite adds the agent and removes the invite. Declining removes the invite. Accepting a notice attachment copies the attachment into the folder the message names. Declining tries to trash an item with the attachment's id.

The two connectors register `IGroupsServicesConnector` and are invisible to the viewer except for latency and staleness.

- Local, name `Groups Local Service Connector` (`GroupsServiceLocalConnectorModule.cs` 72–108). It starts only when groups are enabled and `ServicesConnectorModule` equals that name. Calls go to `GroupsService` in process. There is no HTTP, no shared key, and no `POST /groups-changed`.
- Remote, name `Groups Remote Service Connector`. `MakeRequest` (`GroupsServiceRemoteConnector.cs` 709–726) POSTs form data to `{GroupsServerURI}groups` with `METHOD` set to the operation. `SecretKey` defaults empty and is sent as `X-OpenSim-Groups-Key` only when it is set (`GroupsSecretKey.cs`). With a key set, the process listens for `POST /groups-changed` (`GroupsChangeNotice.cs` 52) and drops the named groups and agents from the cache. Without a key, nothing listens. TB134 already records the redirect case for that header. This plan does not restate it.

The Hypergrid connector is a third `ServicesConnectorModule` name. The shipped region does not load it. Section 5 asks.

Land and object checks read the membership the client already holds, or `IGroupsModule.GetMembershipData`. `HasGroupPowers` (LandManagementModule.cs 1877–1881) requires every requested bit. Phase 2 already reads memberships for the overlay and for properties: P2.6a3a (Robust remote) and P2.6a3a2 (Local SQLite `os_groups`). The owners role in those captures is all 64 bits, which includes `LandDeed`. This slice does not redo that read. It adds the writes, and the cases where a role lacks a land bit.

`CanReturnObjects` (`PermissionsModule.cs` 1736–1775) on group-owned land tests `ReturnGroupOwned`, then `ReturnGroupSet`, then `ReturnNonGroup`, in that order, on the same object. A deeded object is also group-tagged, so one of those bits is not a substitute for the others. That is recorded in section 7 and is not given a TB id here.

On `CreateGroup`, the service creates Everyone, Officers and Owners (`GroupsService.cs` 44–88 and the create path around 200–208). Local runs that in process. Remote runs it on the grid service. The HTTP handler of that service is P1.10.

### Money

`IMoneyModule` (`Source/OpenSim.Framework/IMoneyModule.cs`) is `ObjectGiveMoney`, `GetBalance`, `UploadCovered`, `AmountCovered`, `ApplyCharge` (two overloads), `ApplyUploadCharge`, two `MoveMoney` overloads, `UploadCharge`, `GroupCreationCharge` and `OnObjectPaid`.

The class Tranquillity ships is `SampleMoneyModule`. Its module `Name` is `BetaGridLikeMoneyModule` (`SampleMoneyModule.cs` 239–240). `ReadConfigAndPopulate` (248–284) reads `economymodule`, then `EconomyModule`. An empty or missing name leaves the module enabled. Any other name, including `None`, sets it disabled and it does not register `IMoneyModule`. There is no separate null class. "Null" is that disabled path: callers see no `IMoneyModule`.

`RegionServerDefaults.ini` 2365–2382 comments `EconomyModule` and `SellEnabled`, and says the default module implements enough for free actions. Because the `[Economy]` section exists and the price keys are commented out, the `GetInt` defaults apply: `PriceUpload` 0 (274), `PriceGroupCreate` −1 (282), `PricePublicObjectDecay` 4 (270), `SellEnabled` true (283). The comment at 2381–2382 says the group-create default is 0. The code default is −1.

The module keeps no balances. `doMoneyTransfer` returns true (314–316). `ObjectGiveMoney` calls that and returns it (212–222), then sends a balance update. `UploadCovered` and `AmountCovered` return true. `ApplyCharge`, `ApplyUploadCharge` and the void `MoveMoney` are empty. The bool `MoveMoney` returns true. `GetBalance` is 0. `OnObjectPaid` is not fired.

`UploadCharge` and `GroupCreationCharge` return 0 (110–118) even while `EconomyData` sends `PriceUpload` and `PriceGroupCreate` (698–705). The UDP upload check uses `UploadCharge` (`AssetTransactionModule.cs` 261–271) and, when no module is registered, skips the check. Group create uses `GroupCreationCharge` (GroupsModule.cs 994–1011). That disagreement is TB218. `DTLNSLMoneyModule` returns the configured prices. It registers only when `EconomyModule` equals its name, and it talks to the money server. Both are P1.15. A stock region does not load it.

`ObjectBuy` (799–862), subscribed from the object-buy packet:

- `SellEnabled` false: blue box `Buying is not implemented in this version` (803–807), including a price of 0.
- Price not 0: `Buying anything for a price other than zero is not implemented` (809–812).
- Price 0: the handler looks the part up and then checks sale type, price and sale type again. The alerts are `Unable to buy now. The object was not found.`, `Object {name} is not for sale`, `Object {name} price does not match selected price`, `Object {name} sell type does not match selected type`. A passing buy calls `IBuySellModule.BuyObject`.
- `LocateSceneClientIn` (615–627) returns null when the agent is not a live root, and line 826 uses that scene with no check. Line 827 reads `part.IsRoot` before the null check at 830. A missing scene or a missing local id throws before the not-found alert. That is TB217. Section 5 asks whether Ferroan sends the alert instead.

`ValidateLandBuy` (708–717): when `SellEnabled` is false and the price is above zero, the handler returns and `economyValidated` stays false. Otherwise it sets `economyValidated`. The comment says free transfers always pass. `processLandBuy` (720–728) sets `amountDebited` to the parcel price when the buy was validated, and moves no ledger. Object buy and land buy disagree when `SellEnabled` is false. That is TB220. The ini comment (`RegionServerDefaults.ini` 2375–2376) says the key enables selling for 0.

With no module there is no subscriber and no reply. `EconomyDataRequest` sends the prices from `ReadConfigAndPopulate`, not from the two charge properties. With no module there is no economy packet.

The module also answers the region XML-RPC methods `getCurrencyQuote`, `buyCurrency`, `preflightBuyLandPrep` and `buyLandPrep`. Those answers are the free module's. The money server behind a currency module is P1.15.

## 3. Proving it against C#

Every row closes on a C# result. A Ferroan round trip is not the oracle. The comparison is one of these:

- A capture. Run the viewer packet or the cap against a Pin9 region and keep the datagram, the event-queue body or the HTTP status and body. Ferroan must produce those bytes. Where a fixture redacts a secret, re-encoding the unredacted original has to reproduce the capture before the redaction.
- An oracle. A small compile or a replay of the C# decision (the land-buy predicate, the charge properties, the estate cap, the group-invite refusal) with the same inputs, compared as values.
- A store swap, once P2.9a, P2.0e4.w1, P2.0e4.w2 and P2.0e4.w4 have landed. Edit on Ferroan, stop, open the same file with a Pin9 region, and the viewer sees the same parcel, the same estate lists and the same region settings. The other direction starts from a Pin9 edit. Until those writers exist, the row closes on the packet and on the in-memory land, estate or group the same process would send. The evidence column says which half is in the day and which half waits.
- A live run with `ferroan-bot` or Ferrobot against a Pin9 region, for a packet the bot can send (buy, pass, give, balance, group activate). The captured reply is the fixture. The bot is the sender. It is not the specification.
- Groups Local: the reply packets, and the `os_groups` rows the in-process service wrote, against Pin9's local connector on a copy of the same store.
- Groups Remote: the POST form (`METHOD`, the fields, and the key header only when the key is set) and the `POST /groups-changed` body, against Pin9's remote connector. A C# Robust can sit on the other end of that capture. Ferroan does not reimplement it.

Risks:

- The parcel field matrix is wide, and the UDP update and the cap update do not write the same set. A row that tests one path and claims both will miss `see_avs` and the media fields.
- `CanEditParcelProperties` ignores `allowManager`. Reproducing it means an estate manager's edit fails where the viewer may still offer the control. Section 5 asks before that is locked.
- Estate list updates are queued and stored after the worker is idle. A test that reads the store in the same instant will flake. The caps (20, 500, 63, 500) are part of the result.
- Two group connectors must produce the same viewer packets. The Remote cache and the missing key are part of the Remote row, and they are easy to "simplify" into the Local path.
- Off-region inventory offers wait on P4.30. Building a private sender inside P5.35 would split the instant-message path.
- The shipped money module looks small because it grants free actions and refuses a priced object. The packet prices, the charge properties and `SellEnabled` disagree (TB218, TB220). A row that implements the ini comment instead of the code will not match.
- Restart proof for parcels and estates waits on the Phase 2 writers. That wait is on the calendar. It does not make the protocol day free, and it does not move the writer into this phase.
- A land return that deletes by a new path will disagree with Take on the trash folder and on `KillObject`.
- TB152 stays on the properties send. Storing estate deny bits here does not put them into `ParcelProperties` until that bug's own decision says so.

## 5. Questions for the maintainer

Each one is open. The recommendation is the reading of the pin. It is not a decision.

1. **What does the first Ferroan region ship for money?** The stock region loads `BetaGridLikeMoneyModule`: balance 0, free actions succeed, a priced object buy is refused, a priced land buy is marked paid with no ledger, and naming any other module registers nothing. Recommendation: ship that observable behaviour. Leave `DTLNSLMoneyModule` and the money server on P1.15.

2. **Which group connectors must this slice pass?** Local is in-process. Remote is the POST form. The Robust service is P1.10 either way. Recommendation: prove the viewer packets on Local, and prove the Remote form against Pin9's connector. Do not reimplement the service.

3. **Is the Hypergrid groups connector in this slice?** The example ini names it. The shipped region does not load it. Recommendation: leave it out. A later part can add it beside the Remote form.

4. **Is group chat in this slice?** `MessagingModule` ships unset, so `Groups Messaging Module V2` does not start. Notices are a different path and are P5.28. Recommendation: leave chat out. Notices stay.

5. **Do the estate console commands ship with the region?** They are registered by the estate module the scope names, and they save and resend the handshake. Recommendation: include them (P5.18). A short command throws (TB219). Match the throw until the question below says otherwise.

6. **Does a priced buy wait on a money module that debits?** The shipped module does not debit. With no module, a land buy does not complete and an object buy sends no alert. Recommendation: match those two results. Do not add a ledger in this slice.

7. **Do instant-message inventory offers wait on P4.30?** The copy into a local inventory does not. Delivery to a recipient outside the region does. Recommendation: P5.32 to P5.34 proceed. P5.35 waits on P4.30 and does not grow a second sender.

8. **Does parcel-edit persistence wait on the Phase 2 writers?** D39 leaves the ordinary-path write as the default parcel, and P2.0e4.w1 is the non-default parcel and its access list. Recommendation: do not override D39. Call those writers. Prove packets and in-memory land before they land. Restart proof stays on their far side.

9. **Do script land, money and give calls move into this phase?** They are P4.S6, P4.S7, P4.S9, P4.S14, P4.S15, P4.S19 and P4.F3.5. Recommendation: leave the function bodies there. This file is the region behaviour they call.

10. **TB217.** A buy of a missing local id, or a buy from an agent who is not a live root, throws before `Unable to buy now. The object was not found.` Recommendation: send that alert and do not throw. A throw is not a result a viewer depends on. The other refusal texts stay as Pin9 sends them.

11. **`CanEditParcelProperties` ignores `allowManager`.** Callers pass true for join, divide, freeze, object select and the owner list. The helper passes false (`PermissionsModule.cs` 1405–1410), so an estate manager who is not the owner or an administrator is refused after `JoinDivideAllowed` has let them through. Recommendation: reproduce the ignored argument. Viewers and scripts already see that refusal.

12. **Console water against the viewer water path.** The viewer clamps water to 0..100 and calls `SetWaterLevel` when physics is on and the height changed (`EstateManagementModule.cs` 67–68, 728–748). The console command saves the number it was given and resends the handshake (`EstateManagementCommands.cs` 166–186). Recommendation: reproduce both paths. P3.58 remains the owner of what physics does with the water level afterwards.

## 6. Estimate

39 coder-days, one day for each row above.

What drives it:

- The parcel field matrix, and the UDP update and the cap update writing different fields. P5.1 is the dense day. Subdivide and join are smaller once that write exists.
- Estate lists, the four caps, and the idle delay before `StoreEstateSettings`. The protocol is a day. The store swap waits on P2.0e4.w4 and is not a second implementation.
- The group packet surface over two connectors. Local can be proved in process. Remote is a form compare and needs a Pin9 connector on the other side of the capture. P5.24 and P5.25 are the dense packet rows. If either does not fit a day, the manager splits it before the next row starts.
- Inventory is the copy, the permission masks and the instant message. The off-region half waits on P4.30, so P5.35's calendar is that row's calendar.
- Money is the short end. The shipped module is the free one. The days are the selection, the object-buy alerts, the land-buy flags, and the two disagreements TB218 and TB220.

Restart proofs for parcels, region settings and estate settings wait on P2.9a, P2.9b, P2.0e4.w1, P2.0e4.w2 and P2.0e4.w4. Those waits change when a row can be called finished in a running grid. They do not remove the protocol day, and they do not move the writers into Phase 5.
